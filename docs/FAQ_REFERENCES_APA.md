# TodaGo FAQ online references — APA 7

Dataset revision: 2026-11-19.2. Retrieved/reviewed: 19 November 2026.

The 100-entry collection adapts public FAQ/help topics for TodaGo. The online references below were actually consulted for this revision. The source map records the topic used by each entry; project code separately substantiates the TodaGo instructions. External companies do not endorse TodaGo. Their fees, timelines, insurance, service areas, and other policies have not been imported as TodaGo policy.

No bulk MFAQ or FAQIR records were imported. This is an adapted FAQ collection, not a trained benchmark model or a survey of passenger demand. The previous 33 entries were reviewed against external counterparts and 67 additional entries were added; 99 entries reference operational FAQ/help topics and one entry explains dataset provenance using methodology references.

The list contains 29 FAQ/help or authoritative guidance sources and 3 research papers.

## Source roles and scope

- `angkas_passenger` (faq_collection): Booking steps, pricing explanations, and historical transaction details.
- `angkas_driver_signup` (faq_collection): Driver registration requirements and following up an application; vehicle types, dates, commercial fees, and contact details are not adopted for TodaGo.
- `angkas_consumer` (faq_collection): Choosing services, booking, payment, cancellation, and delivery questions.
- `angkas_help` (faq_collection): Booking, discounts, wallet, route concerns, tracking, technical issues, and post-trip feedback.
- `bsp_qrph` (official_faq): QR Ph basics, participating financial apps, device/internet requirements, merchant checks, scanning, and approval. No publication date is assumed from the PDF's internal document code.
- `mfaq_paper` (research_paper): Related literature about FAQ corpora and multilingual retrieval; no MFAQ records were imported into TodaGo.
- `gcash_missing` (faq_article): Checking transaction history, avoiding immediate retries during processing, retaining the QR reference, and contacting the merchant/provider. Published GCash reversal timelines are not promised by TodaGo.
- `gcash_scan` (faq_article): Using a payment app to scan a merchant QR, review payment details, and authorize payment.
- `datasheets_paper` (research_paper): Dataset origin, collection, composition, intended use, and limitations; not a source of transport answers. [Open-access version](https://arxiv.org/abs/1803.09010).
- `google_permissions` (device_help): Camera, microphone, location, photo, and notification permissions. Device-specific menus can differ.
- `google_location` (device_help): App-specific location access, approximate/precise access, and permission changes.
- `grab_advance` (faq_article): Advance reservation creation, changes, acceptance, and availability. Source text reviewed through indexed Help Centre content; the live page can require JavaScript.
- `grab_group` (faq_article): Group size, stops, fare review, booking notes, tracking, and payment arrangements. Source text reviewed through indexed Help Centre content; the live page can require JavaScript.
- `grab_identity` (faq_article): Identity checks, camera access, photo quality, and retrying verification. Provider-specific processing/privacy promises are not adopted.
- `grab_driver_cancel` (faq_article): Driver cancellation and passenger-requested cancellation. TodaGo eligibility is checked independently.
- `paraphrase_paper` (research_paper): Related literature about query wording and paraphrase-focused retrieval; TodaGo does not implement the paper's trained ranking models.
- `paymongo_events` (technical_documentation): Provider payment outcomes and separate live/test events; supports server-confirmed rather than button-confirmed payment.
- `uber_driver_guide` (help_guide): Pickup/destination navigation, earnings, notifications, and driver activity. Uber payout schedules are not used.
- `uber_cashout` (faq_article): Available earnings, selecting a destination account, confirmation, and payout status. TodaGo uses staff-processed GCash/Maya payouts, not Uber's schedule or instant-transfer promise.
- `uber_pickup` (faq_article): Reviewing GPS suggestions, choosing addresses, and correcting pickup pins before confirmation.
- `uber_contact` (faq_article): Calling or messaging the assigned participant. Uber's number masking and voice readout features are not claimed for TodaGo.
- `uber_lost` (faq_article): Identifying the trip and contacting the appropriate person about missing belongings, without promising recovery.
- `uber_driver` (faq_article): Going online/offline, accepting or declining requests, and taking a break.
- `uber_photo` (faq_article): Adding or updating an account photo. TodaGo's gallery/sync workflow is independently checked.
- `uber_support` (faq_article): Selecting the relevant trip, reviewing its details, submitting an issue, and following up. Uber contact channels and response times do not apply to TodaGo.
- `uber_unavailable` (faq_article): Unavailable drivers, coverage, booking failure, and account/payment checks.
- `uber_signup` (faq_article): Account registration, sign-in trouble, and account credentials. TodaGo's own registration fields and role selection are used.
- `uber_duplicate` (faq_article): Reviewing suspected duplicate charges. Uber authorization-hold rules are not treated as TodaGo behavior.
- `uber_cashout_blocked` (faq_article): A previous pending payout or eligibility limits can prevent another request. TodaGo-specific checks are authoritative.
- `uber_no_requests` (faq_article): Driver availability and troubleshooting lack of incoming requests.
- `uber_fare` (faq_article): Comparing displayed estimates, route distance, stops, and disputed fares. TodaGo's own fare formula takes precedence.
- `uber_rating` (faq_article): Post-trip stars, compliments, and issue feedback. TodaGo rating eligibility and editing limits are separately verified.

## Suggested methodology wording

> The TodaGo FAQ knowledge base was expanded through a purposive review of publicly available transport-service FAQ collections and payment/device guidance. Relevant question topics were paraphrased, deduplicated, grouped by user role, and adapted to TodaGo. Each answer was checked against the implemented application and backend rules. The resulting collection contains 100 entries, including a provenance explanation. Online source identifiers, adaptation topics, access dates, implementation references, and APA citations were retained for traceability.

Use the research papers to discuss FAQ retrieval, paraphrases, and dataset documentation. The studies use methods and corpora different from TodaGo; their reported performance does not measure this app. The local aliases are authored query variants, not samples from those papers. A faculty/domain review and an independent user-query evaluation have not been performed.

## Citation notes

- Undated help pages use `n.d.`; no date is inferred from a crawl timestamp or internal PDF code.
- Same-author, same-date works use alphabetic suffixes by title. Use the same suffix in text.
- Retrieval dates are included for help pages whose content can change.
- Some Grab pages require JavaScript; their indexed Help Centre content was reviewed.
- Text is paraphrased. Whole source pages, customer records, and original benchmark records are not redistributed.
