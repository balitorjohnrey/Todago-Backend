# Panabo City and Carmen service boundaries

The GeoJSON contains the City of Panabo and Carmen, Davao del Norte, from
[geoBoundaries PHL ADM3](https://www.geoboundaries.org/api/current/gbOpen/PHL/ADM3/).
The original sources are NAMRIA, the Philippine Statistics Authority, and
OCHA Philippines. The data is licensed under
[CC BY 3.0 IGO](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/igo/).

The two features were extracted from the simplified release at geoBoundaries
commit `9469f09` (boundary ID `PHL-ADM3-30758251`). The labels were set for the
TodaGo app and coordinates rounded to six decimal places.
